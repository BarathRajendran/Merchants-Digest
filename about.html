About Merchants Digest

Merchants Digest is practical writing, original research and free tools for people who run Shopify stores.

Who writes it

Merchants Digest is written by a former Shopify app founder. I have built and run several Shopify apps. That meant years of reading merchant support tickets, watching which features got used and which got ignored, and seeing first-hand what apps do to a store's speed, checkout and conversion.

That is the angle here. Most advice about Shopify stores comes from people selling something to merchants. I have been on that side, and I know how much of it is noise.

Why there is no name

I currently work at a firm in the ecommerce industry, so I write anonymously. Instead of asking you to trust a name, Merchants Digest shows its work: research comes with its method and limits, and articles link to their sources.

What you will find here

  • Research: we check real Shopify stores and publish what they actually do, without naming them.
  • Tools: free checkers built on the same tests as the research.
  • Articles: guides on conversion, product pages, trust, catalog structure and AI search.
  • Newsletter: a monthly letter on what changed for Shopify merchants and whether it matters.

Independence

Merchants Digest does not take payment to feature apps, tools or stores, and there are no paid placements on the site. If that ever changes, it will be clearly labelled.
