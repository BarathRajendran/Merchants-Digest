Product page checker

Paste one of your Shopify product pages. We check it for the details buyers and AI assistants look for, and show how 90 fashion brands in our research compare.

Works with Shopify stores. We fetch the page once and do not store the address or the results.

What this checks, and what it does not

The checker reads the page the way many search and AI crawlers do: the HTML your store sends before any scripts run. If a detail is added later by an app, it may not be detected here, and it may not be seen by those crawlers either.

It checks one page at a time. Try your best-seller first, then a product that sells less well.
