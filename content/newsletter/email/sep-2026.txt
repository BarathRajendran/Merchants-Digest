CHECKOUT INSIDE GOOGLE AI MODE IS REACHING SHOPIFY STORES

Google has been rolling out checkout inside AI Mode and Gemini, built on its Universal Commerce Protocol. In September, eligible US Shopify merchants started getting emails about it, and reports say enrollment was automatic. You stay the merchant of record, and you can opt out under Sales channels > Agentic.

Meta also appeared as a separate AI channel in the admin on September 8, with products shared through the Shopify Catalog by default.

Neither is good or bad on its own. The question is whether your product data and policies are clear enough to sell without a buyer ever seeing your product page.

"If a buyer can purchase without seeing your page, your product data is your page."

YOUR CONVERSION RATE MAY CHANGE WITHOUT ANYTHING CHANGING

Shopify updated how sessions are counted in analytics later in September, including how some bot traffic is filtered. Session counts, and therefore conversion rates, may not line up with earlier months.

Before you celebrate a jump or panic about a drop, check whether it lines up with the date the change rolled out. Add a note in your own records so next year's comparison makes sense.

SHOPIFY CHANGES WORTH KNOWING

- A redesigned admin started rolling out on September 15 to every store. Things are in slightly different places, but nothing you rely on was removed. https://changelog.shopify.com/
- Variants can now hold up to 20 barcodes, which helps if the same item has different GTINs across suppliers or marketplaces. https://changelog.shopify.com/
- You can collect WhatsApp marketing consent at checkout, and POS can now create local delivery orders. https://www.shopify.com/blog/shopify-retail-roundup-september-2026-v11-15-11-16

TWENTY MINUTES IN YOUR ADMIN

- Open Sales channels > Agentic and check which AI channels can see your products. Make a deliberate choice about each one.
- Make sure every product has a correct barcode or GTIN. AI shopping surfaces use it to match your products.
- Write down the date of the analytics change next to your September numbers.
