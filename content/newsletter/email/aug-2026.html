AI search & discovery

AI-referred orders are growing from a small base

On its second-quarter earnings call, Shopify said traffic and orders coming from AI tools had roughly tripled compared with a year earlier. For most stores that is still a small share of total traffic, but it is the fastest-growing source many of them have.

Shopify also added WebMCP tools to every Liquid storefront on August 5. In plain terms, AI agents now have a standard way to understand and act on your store, and you do not need to set anything up.

Do not reorganise your store around AI. Measure it. The Agentic channel page in your admin shows sessions and orders by assistant.

Small but growing fast is exactly when to start measuring, not when to panic.

Conversion

Your thank-you page changed. Check it.

Non-Plus stores were moved to the new Thank You and Order Status pages on August 26. Anything that ran through the old additional scripts box, such as tracking pixels, post-purchase surveys or upsell code, stops running there.

If your conversion tracking dropped at the end of August, this is the most likely reason. It is not a sales problem. It is a measurement one.

Platform

Shopify changes worth knowing

Thank You and Order Status pages auto-upgraded on August 26 for non-Plus stores. Old additional scripts no longer run on them.

Shopify changelog →

Shopify Managed Markets stopped supporting Delivered Duty Unpaid on August 24. If you ship internationally through Managed Markets, check how duties are now handled for your customers.

POS can now show abandoned online carts and find variants by any associated barcode.

Start holiday prep now, not in November

Black Friday is November 27 this year. The stores that have a calm one make their decisions in August and September.

  • Place a test order and confirm your tracking still fires on the new thank-you page.
  • Decide on your holiday offer now, while you can still think about margin calmly.
  • Note your current AI channel numbers so you can compare them after the holidays.
