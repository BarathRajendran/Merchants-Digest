AI search & discovery

AI agents need facts, not adjectives

Summer '26 leaned heavily into what Shopify calls agentic commerce. By default, your products are shared with AI channels through the Shopify Catalog, so assistants can find and compare them without you doing anything.

What they cannot do is guess. An agent comparing three similar products will lean towards the one with clear sizes, materials, delivery times and return terms. 'Premium quality' tells it nothing. '100% linen, ships in 2 days, 30-day returns' tells it plenty.

An AI agent can only recommend what it can confirm.

Conversion

Answer the question before it becomes a doubt

Stores that convert well rarely have more information than everyone else. They put it earlier. Sizing next to the size selector, delivery next to the button, returns in one line rather than a separate page.

Hesitation builds while a buyer goes looking for an answer. The shorter that search, the fewer people give up.

Platform

Shopify changes worth knowing

Shopify Scripts stop running on June 30. Anything still built on Scripts, such as tiered discounts or hidden shipping rates, will silently stop working. Check before the end of the month, not after.

Make your policy pages pull their weight

Shipping, returns and sizing pages are now read by buyers and by AI systems trying to summarise your store. They are worth an afternoon.

  • Put an estimated delivery date next to the buy button.
  • Summarise your returns policy in one sentence on every product page.
  • Write sizing as text, not only as an image of a chart.
  • Confirm nothing in your store still depends on Scripts.
