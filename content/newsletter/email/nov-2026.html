AI search & discovery

A core update is rolling out during peak season

Google started its November core update on November 10. Rankings may move around for a couple of weeks, right as holiday traffic peaks.

Do not respond by rewriting pages or changing URLs mid-rollout. Let it finish, then compare full weeks. Meanwhile, more buyers are checking deals through AI assistants and price trackers, which reward clear, honest pricing more than clever copy.

During a core update in BFCM week, the best SEO move is usually no move.

Conversion

Clarity beats countdown timers

Holiday buyers are comparing more stores than usual, and they are tired of fake urgency. What actually moves them is certainty: the real discount, the real delivery date, and what happens if the gift does not fit.

If you change only one thing this week, make your delivery cut-off and return window visible near the buy button.

Shopify changes worth knowing

Nothing here needs action this week. These are dates to put in the calendar for January.

Apps must move to expiring offline access tokens before January 1, 2027. If you use custom or private apps, ask whoever built them whether they are ready.

Final checks before Friday

  • Place a full test order with your sale discount applied, on a phone.
  • Check stock levels on your top ten products and set low-stock alerts.
  • Confirm who is watching orders and support messages over the weekend.
  • Freeze theme and app changes until December 1.
