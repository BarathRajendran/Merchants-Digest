How Merchants Digest researches, writes and corrects what it publishes.

Research

  • Every study publishes its sample, method and limitations alongside the results.
  • We do not name the stores we check. Store-level records are kept private, and reports publish totals only.
  • Figures come from what we measured, not from estimates. When a check could miss something, we say so.
  • We only read public pages, with a small number of requests per store.

Sources

  • We link to primary sources, such as Shopify's changelog or Google's own documentation, wherever we can.
  • When information comes from second-hand coverage, or reports disagree, we say who made the claim instead of stating it as fact.
  • We do not invent statistics, quotes or case studies.

How AI is used

We use AI tools to help with research, data processing, drafting and editing. A person decides what is published and checks every claim, and anything that cannot be sourced is cut. Cover images on older articles were generated with AI; newer work uses screenshots, charts and diagrams instead.

Independence

We do not take payment to feature apps, tools or stores. The author writes anonymously because of their current role in the industry; see About.

Corrections

If something we published is wrong, we fix it and note what changed and when. Spotted a mistake? Reply to any newsletter issue.
