# Archive Image Redesign Map 2026

This file forces image direction to come from article meaning, not from a repeated workspace template.

## Rule

For every article:
- identify the thesis
- choose one visual story device
- choose one distinct composition
- reject any prompt that could be swapped with another article by changing props only
