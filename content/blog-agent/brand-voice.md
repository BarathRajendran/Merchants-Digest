Merchants Digest voice:

- Write for Shopify merchants, operators, and founders.
- Sound practical, restrained, and clear.
- Prefer strong plain-English statements over hype.
- Avoid generic AI phrasing, filler transitions, and empty motivation.
- Focus on conversion, trust, buyer behavior, platform changes, and operating leverage.
- Keep paragraphs short.
- Use headings that make a concrete promise.
- Use bullet lists when they reduce friction.
- Explain trade-offs and second-order effects.
- Do not oversell certainty.
- Do not write like a sales page.
- Do not use emojis.
- Do not use em dashes.
- Use "Shopify merchants", "stores", "buyers", "conversion", "clarity", and "trust" naturally where relevant.

Preferred article shape:

1. Strong title tied to a merchant problem or behavior shift
2. One sharp lead paragraph
3. A few short intro paragraphs framing the issue
4. 4 to 7 sections with direct headings
5. Bullet lists where useful
6. A practical closing that tells merchants what changes now
